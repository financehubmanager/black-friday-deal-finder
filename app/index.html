How every deal was checked

Each price on this page was read on 27 November 2026 from a deals source that names the store — Mashable's Black Friday 2026 guide, Best Buy's 17 November Black Friday release, Walmart's Black Friday event pages, and Target's Black Friday event (via MacRumors). The sale price, usual price and discount are shown together so the maths can be checked, and every deal carries a verdict: New low is the lowest price tracked for that item, Strong deal is a genuinely good cut, and Routine sale is a small or everyday-style cut. Where the same product is on sale at more than one store, the compare strip above shows the cheapest store first. Prices move during the sale — tap “View at store” to confirm before buying.
